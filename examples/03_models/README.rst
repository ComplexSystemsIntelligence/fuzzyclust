Clustering for model identification
-----------------------------------

Fuzzy clusters in the product space of inputs and outputs are local models:
Takagi–Sugeno fuzzy models of static functions and of SISO / MIMO dynamic
systems (modified Gath–Geva clustering), switching regression with fuzzy
c-regression models, fuzzy classifiers from supervised clustering, supervised
fuzzy partitions for decision trees, and hinging-hyperplane regression trees.

Visualisation of clustering results
-----------------------------------

Projecting high-dimensional data and cluster prototypes to the plane (PCA,
Sammon mapping, fuzzy Sammon mapping), measuring how faithful the picture is,
and fuzzy self-organizing maps.

Principal component analysis (PCA) chooses the plane that retains as much variance as possible, which says nothing about the distances the clustering algorithm works with. Sammon&#x27;s mapping instead places the N points in the plane so that their pairwise distances d^*_{kl} reproduce the original distances d_{kl}; it minimizes the stress">

A faithful 2-D picture of the partition tells more: whether the clusters are compact groups around their centers, whether two of them should be merged, or whether a cluster merely collects what the others leave. Since the fuzzy Sammon mapping shows the clustering as the algorithm sees it (every prototype becomes a point, and the recomputed memberships match the fitted ones), it is a natural tool for such user-guided validation. This example clusters the Wisconsin breast cancer data (569 samples, the ten standardized &quot;mean&quot; cell-nucleus features, two diagnoses) with FCM for c=2,3,4 and projects every result with FUZZSAM, as in the FUZZ-IEEE paper. It also reports the quality measures of the maps and the convergence of E_\text{fuzz} for several step sizes.">

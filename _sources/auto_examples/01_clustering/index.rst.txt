Clustering algorithms and validation
------------------------------------

Hard and fuzzy partitioning (k-means, k-medoids, FCM, GK, GG), evaluating new
data, choosing the number of clusters and working with pandas / scikit-learn.
Also: generalized fuzzifiers and deterministic annealing, subtractive and
mountain clustering, compatible cluster merging and stability-based
selection of the number of clusters.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Fuzzy c-means turns the hard c-means functional into a fuzzy one by raising the memberships to the power m. This is only one of several mechanisms. Adding an entropy term \lambda\sum_{ik}\mu_{ik}\log\mu_{ik} to the hard functional gives Gaussian-shaped memberships \mu_{ik}\propto\exp(-D^2_{ik}/\lambda); a quadratic term \lambda\sum_{ik}\mu_{ik}^2 gives piecewise linear memberships; and the polynomial fuzzifier of Klawonn and Höppner, g(\mu)=\frac{1-\beta}{1+\beta}\mu^2+\frac{2\beta}{1+\beta}\mu, replaces \mu^m by a function with a positive slope at zero. The last two produce exact zeros: a sample does not belong at all to a cluster on the far side of the data, whereas the FCM membership never vanishes. The entropy functional is also the free energy of deterministic annealing: lowering the temperature \lambda from a large value lets the prototypes split at critical temperatures, which reveals the cluster structure scale by scale.">
